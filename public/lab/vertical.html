The vertical picture, programme by programme (clip/vertical.js, clip/vset.js; docs/publishing/vertical.md)

Each programme's frames composed by the real compositor from synthetic scenes: the presenter's single made tall in the programme's own set, a board on the glass in the set's frame, a map window, the montage, the end card, and PAY ATTENTION's boards in the marquee. Served by the publishing desk's playback server (it serves /api/channel): node tools/publish/playback-server.mjs --port 8797, then /lab/vertical.html?programme=cosmos&scale=2. ?hook=0 shows the master without the clip's hook. window.__vlab.frame(spec) returns one frame as a PNG data URL.
