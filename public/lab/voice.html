Dev tool for the neural voices (server/voice + public/js/voice/player.js). Lists every voiced segment of the queued episodes (GET /api/queue, loopback only; nothing advances the channel) and every advert voice-over (/api/voice/ads), and plays them through the channel's AudioEngine exactly as the director does: speech bus, captions at the recorded word times, the jaw from the clip's loudness envelope. The monitor shows the envelope with the playhead, the sentence with the word being said, the mouth (speechFrame level and viseme) and a white sync mark (top right) lit for 2 frames at every recorded word onset, for A/V checks in recordings. URL: ?autostart=1 plays everything in order; ?src=ads lists the adverts first; ?voices=browser compares with the browser voice. window.__lab: list(), play(i), playAll(), stop(), state().
