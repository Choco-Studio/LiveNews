Composer "lofi" proposal for GLOBIT 24 background music, following docs/programmes/*.md. Live: click a moment (first click unlocks audio); "hold to speak" plays a Kokoro test line and ducks the bed. Offline: window.__audio.render({ programme, moment, seconds, stem, timeline }) for tools/render-audio.mjs (timelines: demo, world-now, tech-bytes, cosmos, money-minute, news-60, break, ident, sombre, standby). Test voices are served from /__voice/ by the composer's own static server; elsewhere a speech-shaped stand-in is used.
