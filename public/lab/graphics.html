Dev tool for public/js/graphics. Every frame is a pure function of t: a fresh Graphics replays the scenario timeline at 120 Hz up to t, with a frozen clock (15:42 London). window.__lab.render(t, opts?) draws instant t; window.__lab.set({ scenario, bg, theme, replay, kicker, breaking, caption }). URL params use the same names. Backgrounds: neutral, photo (bright picture), wide / close (real studio shots, drawn by studio.js), montage (cards.js).
