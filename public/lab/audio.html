Dev tool for public/js/audio. Every tune and cue is rendered offline (OfflineAudioContext, 48 kHz) with the channel's own synth and mixer, then measured: waveform with the -1 dBFS ceiling, BS.1770 momentary loudness against the channel target, spectrogram, and an envelope probe (every oscillator replaced by a constant) whose per-sample steps reveal clicks. Other views: the signature, the loudness of every tune, ducking under speech, the viseme timeline of a sentence, TTS re-sync and the blip voice. window.__lab.render(i) draws view i (async); window.__lab.stats() returns the numbers; window.__lab.views lists the views.
