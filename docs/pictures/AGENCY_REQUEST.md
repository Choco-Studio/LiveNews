**Subject:** Quote request: editorial news stills for a 24/7 online news channel (about 1,200 a month)

Hello,

I run GLOBIT 24, a 24-hour English-language news channel published on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram (and also on
Facebook and X). The channel is produced with AI assistance: software drafts and voices the scripts, and we label the
channel as AI-assisted. We would like a quote for an editorial licence for news still photographs.

What we need:

- about **1,200 still photographs a month** (about 40 a day), news and current affairs only;
- shown inside our videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, in 16:9 and 9:16;
- each picture credited on screen while it is shown (for example "REUTERS/JANE DOE") and in the video's description;
- the videos are monetised through the platforms' own advertising; your pictures are never used in advertising, as
  an endorsement, or in thumbnails unless you agree.

Our visual style: every picture is shown in a **pixel-art treatment**. It is reduced to 416 × 234 pixels and a
24-colour palette, then enlarged in blocks, and cropped to 16:9 or 9:16. We never change what a picture shows or its
editorial meaning, and we never combine it with other pictures.

Please quote with these changes to your standard terms:

1. **Alteration.** Written permission to show your pictures in the pixel-art treatment described above, and to crop
   them to 16:9 and 9:16, as long as their editorial meaning is not changed.
2. **Repeat use.** Each picture may be used in any number of our programmes for 7 days after it is first used, and may
   stay in videos already published.
3. **All five platforms under one licence**, not priced per platform, and the rights do not end because a platform's
   own terms give it a licence to host our uploads.
4. **Monetised videos.** Your pictures may appear in videos that earn platform advertising revenue. We will not
   register your pictures with any platform's content-matching system or claim them as ours.
5. **Credit instead of a watermark** on social platforms: the on-screen credit and the description line, because a
   watermark does not survive the pixel treatment.
6. **AI.** We do not use your pictures to train or tune any AI model, and we run no automated analysis on them. Please
   confirm that an AI-assisted channel may show your licensed pictures on these terms.
7. **Withdrawn pictures.** Tell us how a withdrawn ("kill") picture must be handled in videos already published. We
   can stop using it in new programmes within 2 hours.

Please send the monthly price (or the price per picture), the minimum term, and your licence with these changes
marked. A call is fine too.

Thank you,

[name]
GLOBIT 24
[email and phone]

## Before sending

- **Volume.** 1,200 a month is about 40 pictures a day: each programme's lead and its top three stories. Fewer is
  cheaper; the channel can mix wire pictures for the leads with the cleared open-licence pictures for the rest
  (`docs/pictures/RIGHTS.md`).
- **The face reader.** The desk reads the expression of open-licence portraits with a model before a grave story may
  show them (RIGHTS.md §4). It must never read an agency picture (point 6 of the email): a wire picture is chosen
  for its story by the agency's caption, not by the face reader.
- **What the lawyer should see first** (RIGHTS.md §8): the signed order, before the first wire picture airs.
