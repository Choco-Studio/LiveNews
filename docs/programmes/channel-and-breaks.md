# CHANNEL IDENTITY, IDENTS, BUMPERS AND AD BREAKS: style bible

Idents, continuity cards, the UP NEXT promo, the top-of-hour countdown and parody ads. Builds on `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md` and `world-now.md`.

Research, 2026-10-02: the proxy blocked direct fetches of non-GitHub hosts (YouTube thumbnails included), so claims come from search summaries of the linked pages. Unsourced rules say "our rule".

## 1. Identity

Between programmes GLOBIT 24 should feel like a public-service channel at 3 a.m.: calm, beautifully lit, precise, and faintly amused that you are still up.

- Idents are small observational films of an adult pixel world.
- Continuity cards talk to the viewer in deadpan sentences.
- Ads carry the satire. Each is an exact replica of a real commercial genre, selling an absurd product with total sincerity.

It is for grown-ups who leave the channel on in a corner. The joke is never that the channel is cute. The joke is that the craft is real.

## 2. Real references

## 3. Our style

Variety is deliberate: each break element has its own grammar. Palette, signature and timing unify them.

### Delivery and writing
**Continuity voice** (our rule): an unseen announcer, an en-GB Kokoro voice no presenter uses. 150–160 wpm, slower than the anchors, in sentences of 4–12 words. "This is GLOBIT 24." at most once per break.

**Continuity cards**, written live by the server writer:
- **Input:** `next.title`, presenters, London time, the next lead headline if neutral.
- **Form:** 2–4 cards of 8 words or fewer. Statements only, no "!", at most one pun per break. The last card is plain information.
- **Targets:** joke about the channel, the hour or the viewer. Never about a story's subject or a real person. No wit after a grave lead.
- **Fallback:** about 20 validated evergreen sets.
- **Style examples:** `NEWS IN 60 IS NEXT. / IT LASTS A MINUTE. / WE CHECKED.` and `IT IS 03:00 IN LONDON. / YOU ARE STILL HERE. / SO ARE WE.`

**Spoken forms:** "at nine". "London time" at most hourly. Programme names in words ("News in Sixty").

**Ads:** an absurd product, read sincerely, never winking. Numbers get absurd precision ("ninety-nine point nine recurring per cent"). Warnings go Happy Fun Ball style: 3–5, each darker, on one level tone.

### Structure (live from the break item, seeded by `break.id`)

| # | Segment | Driven by | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Network stinger | programme outro | 0.8 s |
| 2 | Cards, or a short ident (alternating; the ident after a grave lead) | server cards / fallback | 6–10 s |
| 3 | Ads × `break.ads` (`pickAds`) | ad templates | 20–30 s each |
| 4 | UP NEXT promo | `next`, `THEME_ACCENT` | 6–8 s |
| 5 | Main ident plus living hold; the voice names the programme | London daypart, seed | 8–12 s + 3–20 s |
| 6 | Hard cut to the open | — | — |

- **If the queue is late:** the hold stretches to 20 s, then a card set plays, then an extra ad. Never a frozen frame.
- **Crossing :00 London time:** a 30 s countdown replaces segment 5, and its 0:00 is the open's first frame.

### Set and light
**Idents**
- **Dayparts (London):** night 22–06, morning 06–12, day 12–18, evening 18–22.
- **Scenes:** ordinary adult places: a night-bus shelter in rain, a launderette at 02:00, a rooftop water tower, a ferry at dawn, an empty newsroom with one lamp.
- **The mark** comes from the scene (Channel 4 grammar): a round object plus a small warm square light at its top-right align into the globe-and-bit for at least 1.2 s.
- **Light:**
  - Cool key from above left: `P.navy`/`P.slate` at night, `P.steel`/`P.fog` by day.
  - Only one warm practical (`P.cream`/`P.yellow`, or the next programme's accent) and the mark exceed L\* 70.
  - At most three shades per object. Figures 6–7 heads tall, often silhouettes.

**Cards:** `P.black`, nothing else.

**Ads:** each copies one genre exactly (our rule, from references 4 and 5).

| Genre (brand) | Light and set | Cuts | Voice-over | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Luxury (BitFizz Reserve, Grand Buffer) | `P.black` void, 1 px `P.silver` rims, fill fades in over 1.5 s for the reveal | 3–5 s, dither dissolves | 110–120 wpm, ≤25 words | `pad` + low `tri`, 60–72 BPM; 1–2 s of silence before the brand |
| Keynote (Cloudbrella, ScreechNet) | `P.ink` stage, turntable product, specs in `P.fog` | 3–4 s on bar lines | 140–150 wpm, calm superlatives | `pluck` ostinato, 100–110 BPM |
| Insurance (SafeSector) | warm kitchen, `P.cream` window key, adults; the disk is a prop | 2–3 s | 150 wpm; legal at 200+ | `pluck` + `softtri`, 88–96 BPM |
| Athlete (Hi-Res Gym) | `P.black`/`P.steel`/`P.white`, hard side key | 0.8–2 s, then a 4 s held breath | ≤15 words | `timpani` heartbeat, silence drop |
| Artisan food (Corners) | macro tabletop, warm `P.orange` key | 2–4 s | 120 wpm | sparse `bell` |
| Public information film (filler) | four-colour period grade | 4–6 s holds | austere, 130 wpm | detuned `sine` |

### Camera and directing
- **Ident:** one continuous 8–12 s dolly or orbit, eased, with no cuts.
  - Parallax speeds: foreground up to 12 px/s, middle 6, background 2. Each layer snaps to whole pixels.
  - Lock off on the alignment. In the hold, at most two ambient motions (rain, a page, a light changing one palette step).
- **Ads:** one move per shot (push, pull, turntable or slow track) or none. No zoom punches. At most one whip pan, athlete ads only. Turntables 15–25°/s, under 180° per shot.
- **Comic timing:** after an absurd line, at least 0.8 s of stillness.
- **Gestures:** full arcs (anticipation, action, settle), at most one per shot.
- **Cards:** no camera. Each card cuts in and stays 1.2 s + 0.25 s per word, clamped to 1.8–3.2 s.
- **Data:** `break.id` seeds the scene, ad order and cards. The London hour sets the daypart, `next.programId` the accent, and the previous lead's emotion gates wit.

### Graphics
- **Tokens:** field `P.black`/`P.ink`; type `P.white` > `P.silver` > `P.fog`; rules `P.red` 1–2 px; `P.yellow` only on the logo's bit; `THEME_ACCENT` only in the promo and the ident practical.
- **Card:** 5x7 at 2x in white, left-aligned at x=40, centred near y=100. Up to 26 characters and 2 lines, otherwise 1x. Micro `21:00 LONDON` in `P.fog`.
- **Ident wordmark:** appears 0.4 s after the alignment as a two-step palette fade (`P.steel` then `P.white`, 0.3 s). No slide, no scale.
- **Promo:** the next open's final lock-up frame (opens are templates), a micro `NEXT · 21:00`, and a `P.silver` teaser of 45 characters or fewer.
- **End slate:** wordmark at 2x with +1 px tracking, tagline at 1x, URL in micro `P.fog`, legal in micro `P.steel` (up to 2 lines, at least 3 s). Hold at least 3.5 s, with one glint as the only motion.
- **Motion:** in over 0.3 s with ease-out quint. Out over 0.2 s, or cut. Travel at most 8 px. Never `easeOutBack`.

### Music and sound (motif from `audio/themes.js`)
- **Ident:** the motif once on `pulse12` with echo, over a `pad` and `tri` pedal, at 84–92 BPM by night and 96–104 by day. The last note lands on the alignment. The hold loops `pad` + `pluck`.
- **Countdown:** a quiet `bell` tick every second, `tri` eighths at 120 BPM, and a `pad` that turns minor to major at 0:00. Lowe's grammar, not his notes.
- **Cards:** an 80–88 BPM loop of `tri` bass and swung `pluck`.
- **Promo:** the existing `next` sting.
- **Silence:** 0.3 s of true silence between ads and before the ident. Luxury and athlete ads may hold silence for more than 1 s before the brand.
- **Mix:** beds sit at least 18 dB under voice. Ads and idents stay within ±1 LU of the programmes (our rule).
- **Banned:** boings, slide whistles, cartoon pops, laugh tracks, bouncy pentatonic jingles.

### Transitions
- **Into the break:** the 0.8 s stinger, re-graded to `P.ink`/`P.black` slabs with a 1 px `P.red` edge. The yellow slab in `STINGER_LAYERS` goes.
- **Between ads**, and from cards into ads or from ads into the promo: 0.3 s of black and silence. No stingers, unlike today's `playBreak`.
- **Out of the break:** cut from the promo to the ident, then hard-cut on the downbeat from the ident's lock-off to the open's navy field.

### Do / Don't
- **Do:** copy the genre exactly; light with one source plus edge light, and keep real darkness; put jokes in copy and small print, then hold still; use adult proportions and seeded variety; keep the good premises and legal lines ("NOT EFFECTIVE AGAINST ACTUAL RAIN").
- **Don't (childish or cheap):** sunbursts (today in SafeSector, Corners, Cloudbrella); objects with faces (Flop, the cloud); onomatopoeia (BONK!, FWOMP!, BOING!); exclamation captions (COVERED!, INSURED!); blush cheeks; child protagonists; full-frame saturated cyan or yellow; overshoot easing; stacked puns; a laughing voice-over.

## 4. Acceptance checklist
1. No break frame or ad script has an object with a face, onomatopoeia or "!".
2. No radial rays. Outside end slates, saturated colours cover at most 15% of pixels.
3. Each ident is one move, zero cuts, mark aligned at least 1.0 s, then a lock-off. At most two things move in the hold.
4. 20 seeded breaks show at least 4 ident scenes, no back-to-back repeat. Night mean L\* is at least 8 below day.
5. Cards: at most 2 lines and 8 words, white on `P.black`, 1.8–3.2 s each.
6. Word timings: continuity voice 150–160 wpm, luxury ads 125 wpm or less.
7. 0.2–0.4 s of black and silence between ads. No music or stinger crosses an ad boundary.
8. Ads within ±1 LU of programmes. Beds at least 18 dB under voice.
9. From three stills, a critic can name the genre each ad copies.
10. End slates hold at least 3.5 s with a static wordmark. Legal readable for at least 3 s.
11. Every non-filler break with `next` has the promo in its `THEME_ACCENT`, with names exactly as in `channel.json`.
12. No yellow stinger slab. The ident cuts to the open on the downbeat. The countdown ends on the open's first frame.
13. After a grave lead, no witty card.
14. Full figures 6–7 heads tall, and gestures show their in-betweens.
