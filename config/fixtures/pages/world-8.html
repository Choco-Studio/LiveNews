Storm forces ferry cancellations across the Greek islands

Strong winds have forced ferry operators to cancel sailings between Athens and several Greek islands. Coast guard officials expect services to resume on Friday. Passengers waiting at the port of Piraeus were offered places on later sailings. Gusts reached gale force in the Aegean overnight. Port authorities say no vessels have been damaged.

Winds of up to 9 on the Beaufort scale were forecast in the Aegean Sea. Some islands rely on the ferries for food deliveries and fuel. Passengers were advised to check with operators before travelling to the port.
