Iceland volcano: lava fountains light up the Reykjanes sky

Lava fountains up to 50 metres high lit up the night sky over the Reykjanes peninsula. Nearby roads were closed as a precaution, officials said. Roads near the eruption site have been closed to sightseers.

The eruption is on a peninsula south-west of the capital, Reykjavik. Scientists say the lava is flowing away from nearby towns. The national airport says flights are not affected.
