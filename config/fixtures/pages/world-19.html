Dublin hospitals under pressure as flu cases rise

Hospitals in Dublin say their emergency departments are under severe pressure after a rise in flu cases. Health officials say 600 patients were waiting for a bed on Monday, the highest figure this year.

Some planned operations have been postponed to free up beds. Doctors are asking people with mild symptoms to rest at home and to get a flu vaccine.

Health officials expect flu cases to peak in the next two weeks.
