Heavy rain floods streets in coastal towns of Kerala

Heavy monsoon rain has flooded streets in several coastal towns in Kerala, India. Rain fell for most of the night, and water rose quickly in low-lying streets near the coast.

Local authorities have opened 120 relief camps and closed schools for two days. Boats were used to help people reach dry ground in some neighbourhoods.

Forecasters expect more heavy rain over the next three days. Officials have asked people to avoid travel where roads are under water.
