Wildfire near Marseille forces thousands to evacuate

Firefighters are battling a wildfire on a pine ridge north of Marseille. The fire spread quickly in strong winds during the afternoon.

About 3,000 residents have been moved from three villages, local officials say, and a motorway has been closed. Water-bombing planes are flying over the ridge, and hundreds of firefighters are working on the ground.

Officials have asked people nearby to keep their windows closed because of the smoke.
