Lisbon opens a new riverside tram line

Lisbon has opened a new tram line along the Tagus river. The city says the 9 kilometre route will carry 40,000 passengers a day and cut car traffic in the old town. “This line will change how people move around the old town,” the mayor said. It is the first new tram route in the city for more than 20 years. Trams will run every six minutes at peak times.

The trams run every six minutes during the day and every fifteen minutes at night. Building the line took four years, and parts of the riverside road were closed during the works. Shop owners along the route say they hope the stops will bring more customers to the waterfront.
