Panama Canal reopens after a day-long closure

The Panama Canal has reopened to ships after fog closed it for a day. Thick fog had forced the canal authority to stop all crossings, a rare step for the waterway.

Ships began moving again at dawn, with pilots guiding the first vessels through the locks at reduced speed. The canal authority says about 30 ships are waiting to cross.

The canal carries around 5 percent of world sea trade, so even a short closure can delay deliveries. Shipping companies say the delays should clear within days.
