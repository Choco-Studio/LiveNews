Germany rail strike leaves stations empty

Stations across Germany were almost empty on Thursday as rail workers began a 24-hour strike over pay. The operator says only one in five long-distance trains is running. Freight trains were also affected, the operator says.

The strike is the third this year in a long dispute over pay and working hours. Many commuters switched to buses and car shares for the day. The union says it will announce its next steps after the strike ends.
