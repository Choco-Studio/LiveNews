Rice prices ease in Asia after strong harvests

Rice prices in Asia have fallen 8 percent since July after strong harvests in Thailand and Vietnam. Thailand's harvest was its largest in five years, helped by steady rains during the growing season.

Vietnam also reported higher yields after farmers planted new varieties. Traders say supplies look healthy for the rest of the year.

Lower prices are good news for households in countries where rice is the main food. Some farmers' groups warn that falling prices could squeeze their incomes. Exporters expect shipments to rise next month.
