Marseille wildfire: thousands evacuate from hill villages

A wildfire north of Marseille has forced about 3,000 people to leave three hill villages, officials say. Firefighters say strong winds are making the fire hard to contain. Evacuated families are staying in schools and sports halls in nearby towns.

The fire started in the afternoon and spread quickly through dry pine forest. Water-bombing planes have been flying over the hills since the early evening. Officials have asked people nearby to keep their windows closed because of the smoke.
