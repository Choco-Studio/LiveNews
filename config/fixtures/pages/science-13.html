Glacier in the Alps shrinks by 3 percent in one summer

Measurements show a large glacier in the Alps lost 3 percent of its volume this summer. The measurements were made with drones and surveys on the ice at the end of the summer.

Scientists say the ice is now thinner than at any time since records began. Researchers say warm nights, not only hot days, have sped up the melting.

A lake has formed at the foot of the ice where there was none twenty years ago. Mountain guides have moved some routes away from the ice because of falling rocks.
