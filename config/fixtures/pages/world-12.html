Moderate earthquake shakes northern Chile, no damage reported

A magnitude 5.8 earthquake shook northern Chile on Tuesday morning. Emergency services say there are no reports of damage or injuries. The quake was felt in the port city of Antofagasta. Chile sits on one of the most active seismic zones in the world.

The earthquake struck about 40 kilometres below the ground, which reduced the shaking at the surface. Buildings in the region are built to strict rules because large earthquakes are common. Chile's navy said there was no risk of a tsunami.
