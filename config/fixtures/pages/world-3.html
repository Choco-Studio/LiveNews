Iceland volcano erupts again on the Reykjanes peninsula

A fissure eruption has started again on the Reykjanes peninsula in Iceland. Scientists say lava is flowing away from nearby towns and flights are not affected. It is the latest in a series of eruptions in the area since 2021. The fissure is about 3 kilometres long, according to the meteorological office. A nearby spa has closed as a precaution.

The eruption began late in the evening, when a fissure about 3 kilometres long opened in the ground. Scientists from the meteorological office are flying over the site to measure how fast the lava moves. Residents of a nearby town were moved as a precaution during an earlier eruption in the same area.
