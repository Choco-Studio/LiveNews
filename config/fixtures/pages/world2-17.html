Seville records 44 degrees as Spain issues heat alert

Temperatures reached 44 degrees Celsius in Seville on Wednesday, the highest of the summer. Spain’s weather agency has issued a red alert for the south and asked people to avoid going out in the afternoon. Some schools have moved lessons to the early morning.

The weather agency says the heat will last at least until the weekend. Doctors advise drinking water often and staying in the shade. Some outdoor work has been moved to the early morning.
