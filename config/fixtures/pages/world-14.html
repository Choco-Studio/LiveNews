Seoul festival lights up the Han river with 1,000 drones

A festival in Seoul lit up the sky over the Han river with a show of 1,000 drones designed by local artists. Organisers said about 200,000 people watched from the riverbanks. The drones formed the shape of a whale above the water.

The show lasted about fifteen minutes and was timed to music. Organisers said the drones were controlled by a team of six pilots from the riverbank. The festival runs for three more nights.
