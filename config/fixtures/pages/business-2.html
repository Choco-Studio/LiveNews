Bank of Canada holds interest rates at 3.5 percent

The Bank of Canada has kept its main interest rate unchanged at 3.5 percent. The decision was expected by most economists, who had forecast no change at this meeting.

Inflation in Canada slowed to 2.4 percent last month, close to the bank's 2 percent target. Policymakers in Ottawa said inflation is easing but they want more evidence before cutting.

Mortgage lenders said fixed rates are unlikely to move much before the next decision in December. The Canadian dollar was little changed after the announcement.
