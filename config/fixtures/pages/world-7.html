Paris unveils plans to plant 170,000 trees by 2030

The city of Paris has announced plans to plant 170,000 trees by 2030 to cool the city in summer. Squares near the Seine will be the first to change. The city says shade can make streets several degrees cooler on the hottest days.

The plan also turns some car lanes into planted walkways. Schoolyards will be opened to the public on hot afternoons as shaded spaces. The city says work on the first squares will start next spring.
