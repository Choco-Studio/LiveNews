Coral recovers on parts of the Great Barrier Reef off Queensland

Scientists say coral cover has grown on parts of the Great Barrier Reef for a second year. “The reef is showing it can bounce back when it gets a break,” a lead researcher said. Researchers warn that another hot summer could undo the gains.

The measurements come from surveys of more than a hundred reefs along the coast of Queensland. Scientists say fast-growing corals have recovered quickest. The reef stretches for more than 2,000 kilometres.
