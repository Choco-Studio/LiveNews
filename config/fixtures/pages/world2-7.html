Mexico City closes its historic centre to cars on Sundays

Mexico City will close the streets of its historic centre to cars every Sunday from next month. The city says walkers and cyclists will get 15 kilometres of open road.

The plan follows a trial last year on a few streets near the main square. Street vendors and cafés will be allowed to use more space on Sundays. Residents and delivery vans will still be able to enter at set times.
