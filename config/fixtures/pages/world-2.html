Kenya switches on its largest solar farm near Nairobi

A solar farm north of Nairobi has started supplying power to the national grid. Officials say it can light around 300,000 homes and is the biggest in East Africa so far. Its panels cover an area the size of about 400 football pitches. Engineers say the farm will feed the grid even during the dry season, when hydropower falls. A second phase is planned for next year.

The plant was built in two years and employs about 300 people, most of them from nearby towns. Kenya already gets much of its power from geothermal plants in the Rift Valley. Engineers say the farm will supply the most power in the dry season, when hydropower falls.
