Norway raises interest rates in a surprise move

Norway’s central bank has unexpectedly raised its main interest rate to 4.75 percent. Most economists had expected no change. Mortgage rates are expected to follow within weeks.

The rise is the bank's first move in more than a year. The central bank said prices are rising faster than it expected. The Norwegian krone strengthened after the announcement.
