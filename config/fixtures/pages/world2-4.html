Venice raises its sea barriers in a test before the autumn tides

Venice has raised its sea barriers for a test ahead of the autumn high tides. Engineers say the 78 gates can hold back a tide of up to 3 metres. The gates lie flat on the seabed until they are needed.

The barriers were first used in 2020 and have protected the city from several high tides since. Each gate takes about thirty minutes to rise. Some shop owners in St Mark's Square say even small floods still reach their doors.
