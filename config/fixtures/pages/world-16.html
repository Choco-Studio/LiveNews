Hurricane Elena makes landfall on Mexico’s Yucatán coast

Hurricane Elena has made landfall near Cancún with winds of 180 kilometres per hour. Mexico’s civil protection agency says about 1.2 million homes are without power and 40,000 people have gone to shelters. “This is a life-threatening situation,” the agency said.

The storm weakened slightly as it moved inland over the Yucatán peninsula. Airports in Cancún and Mérida have closed, and hotels have moved guests to inner rooms. Forecasters expect heavy rain over the next two days, with a risk of flooding in low areas.
