Peru archaeologists uncover a 3,000-year-old temple in the Andes

Archaeologists in Peru say they have found the remains of a temple about 3,000 years old in the northern Andes. The temple sits on a hillside above a river valley.

The team found painted walls and a ceremonial stairway. The walls are painted in red and yellow, and the stairway leads to a raised platform.

The archaeologists say the site may have been used for ceremonies for several centuries. Excavation will continue until the rainy season begins.
