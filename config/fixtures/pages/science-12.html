Giant tortoises return to a Galápagos island after a century

Rangers have released 50 young giant tortoises on an island in the Galápagos where the species vanished about 100 years ago. The tortoises were carried across the island in wooden crates and released near a freshwater pond.

The young tortoises were bred at a centre on another island, where eggs were kept warm until they hatched. “It is a homecoming,” a park ranger said.

Rangers will track them with small radio tags. Park staff say it may take decades before the tortoises breed on their own.
