Brazil reports a sharp fall in Amazon deforestation

Brazil's space agency says deforestation in the Amazon fell by 30 percent over the past twelve months. Satellite images show the lowest level of clearing in a decade. Officials credit more inspections and fines for illegal clearing. Environmental groups welcomed the figures but say clearing is still rising in some states. The agency publishes its estimates every year.

The figures cover the twelve months to the end of July. Environmental groups welcomed the news but said clearing is still too high in some states. The government has promised to end illegal deforestation by 2030.
