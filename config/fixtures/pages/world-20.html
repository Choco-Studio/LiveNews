Turin car plant closure threatens 2,400 jobs

A carmaker has confirmed it will close its plant near Turin by the end of next year. Unions say 2,400 jobs are at risk and have called a strike for Friday. Regional officials say they will meet the company next week.

The plant has built cars for more than fifty years and is one of the largest employers in the area. The company says demand for the models made there has fallen sharply. Unions want the regional government to find a new owner for the site.
