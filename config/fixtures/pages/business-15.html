Rail strike halts most trains across Germany

A 24-hour strike by rail workers has halted most long-distance trains across Germany. Stations in Berlin, Munich and Frankfurt were almost empty during the morning rush hour.

The operator says only one in five services is running. Regional trains in some areas were less affected.

The union wants higher pay and shorter shifts for train crews, and unions say talks over pay have stalled. No new talks have been scheduled. Many commuters switched to buses and car shares for the day.
